## Process Flow

1. Campaign or exhibition activity is planned.
2. Source and outreach execution is tracked.
3. Performance and source quality are reviewed.
4. Learnings are used to refine future lead-generation actions.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start in Marketing Desk and review live campaigns, mailers, exhibitions, and any source-performance signals needing action.
2. Use Campaigns and Exhibitions to keep planning, execution status, and follow-up ownership in one place.
3. Review Source Performance before repeating an activity so Josh becomes the reference point for what is actually producing better leads.
4. Use Marketing Mailers from Josh-linked context so outreach, activity status, and future learning stay connected.
5. Capture weak channels, campaign blockers, and quality-of-lead observations in Josh so future sales handoffs improve.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review active campaigns and event plans.
2. Track mailers and marketing execution status.
3. Check source performance and response quality.
4. Flag insights that should improve future sales pipeline inputs.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for campaign activity that is running without clear ownership, status, or result visibility in Josh.
- Check weak source performance before repeating spend or outreach effort.
- Keep campaign observations and lead-quality concerns tied to Josh records instead of separate notes.
- Capture improvement ideas around campaign visibility and sales handoff quality during live use.
