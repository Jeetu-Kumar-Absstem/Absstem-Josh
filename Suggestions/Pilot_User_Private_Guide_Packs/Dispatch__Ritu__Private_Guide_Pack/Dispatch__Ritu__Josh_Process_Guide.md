## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to control fulfilment readiness, shipment execution, and dispatch-side operational completion after order confirmation.

## Primary Screens

- Dispatches
- Sales Orders
- Stock Ledger

## Process Flow

1. Confirmed sales order enters fulfilment stage.
2. Dispatch readiness is reviewed against stock and document state.
3. Shipment is executed and recorded.
4. Operational handoff from order to shipment is completed.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start with dispatch-ready orders and shipments that are pending document, stock, or execution clearance.
2. Use Sales Orders to confirm that the order is commercially ready before shipment movement begins.
3. Check Dispatches and Stock Ledger together when availability, picking, or shipment completion needs confirmation.
4. Update shipment status in Josh as execution happens so the order-to-dispatch trail remains complete.
5. If stock, documents, or upstream approval is missing, keep the blocker visible in Josh before dispatching.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review dispatch-ready orders.
2. Track stock and shipment readiness.
3. Complete dispatch documentation and execution.
4. Flag missing material or commercial blockers before shipment.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for orders marked ready to ship while stock, documents, or commercial clearance are still incomplete.
- Check shipment execution status in Josh instead of relying on verbal confirmation alone.
- Avoid dispatching from an offline list that is not aligned with the live sales-order status.
- Capture repeated fulfilment or shipment-visibility issues as pilot feedback.
