## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to manage inward handling, stock visibility, stock movement control, and support material issue / return flow for operations.

## Primary Screens

- Goods Receipts
- Stock Ledger
- Stock Adjustments
- Stock Transfers
- Job Cards

## Process Flow

1. Incoming material is received and recorded.
2. Inventory becomes visible in Stock Ledger.
3. Transfers and adjustments are documented.
4. Material is issued and returned against operational jobs.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start with inward receipts, stock movement tasks, and material issue or return activity pending in Josh.
2. Use Goods Receipts to record incoming material correctly so stock becomes visible to the rest of the flow immediately.
3. Check Stock Ledger before issuing, transferring, or adjusting material so the transaction is based on current system stock.
4. Support Job Cards from the same Josh flow when material must be issued or returned for production work.
5. Keep adjustments, shortages, and stock movement exceptions in Josh so downstream desks work from one inventory truth.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review inward pending posting.
2. Monitor stock movement and balance visibility.
3. Track adjustments and transfers needing action.
4. Support job-card issue and return flow accurately.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for receipt delays, unposted stock movement, or issue-return gaps that weaken inventory accuracy.
- Check stock before every issue, adjustment, or transfer so the transaction reflects the current system balance.
- Avoid maintaining a side record of stock movement when the same trace should sit inside Josh.
- Capture repeated inventory-visibility or movement-control gaps as pilot feedback.

Josh helps Stores keep inward, stock movement, and issue-return control aligned to one inventory record.
