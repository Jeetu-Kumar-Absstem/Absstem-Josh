## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to manage customer-facing commercial flow from enquiry visibility through quote creation, order confirmation, and dispatch handoff readiness.

## Primary Screens

- Sales Desk
- Leads
- Customers
- Quotes
- Sales Orders
- Dispatches

## Process Flow

1. Lead or customer requirement is captured and tracked.
2. Commercial discussion is converted into a quote.
3. Approved or won quote is converted into a sales order.
4. Sales order is checked for fulfilment readiness before dispatch.
5. Dispatch execution completes the commercial handoff.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start in Sales Desk and review the full pipeline, pending quotes, and orders waiting for commercial follow-up.
2. Open Leads and Customers to confirm that the account, enquiry, and decision-maker details are complete before pushing the case ahead.
3. Use Quotes to refine pricing, commercial terms, and approval readiness so the team works from one current quote record only.
4. Move winning business into Sales Orders and track dispatch readiness from Josh instead of asking for separate status updates offline.
5. Use Josh as the control view for coaching, escalation, and closure so open deals, pending approvals, and handoff gaps stay visible.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review open leads and active customer opportunities.
2. Prepare and refine quotes.
3. Track quotes awaiting approval or conversion.
4. Monitor sales orders ready for dispatch handoff.
5. Check delivery status and cross-desk blockers.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch quotes that are commercially active but not moving toward order conversion.
- Check that approvals, pricing exceptions, and dispatch handoff gaps are visible in Josh before the team escalates offline.
- Watch for poor pipeline hygiene such as stale leads, incomplete customer context, or duplicate commercial follow-up.
- Capture pilot feedback around visibility, team control, and conversion discipline in the same guide set.

## Simple One-Line Summary

Josh gives Sales leadership one connected view of pipeline control, quote movement, and order handoff readiness.
