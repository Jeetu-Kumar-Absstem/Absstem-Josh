## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to validate financial document flow around invoicing, receipts, receivable visibility, banking, and transaction closure.

## Primary Screens

- Invoices
- Receipts
- AR Desk
- GL Desk
- Bank / Cash Book
- Sales Returns

## Process Flow

1. Commercial billing is raised through invoices.
2. Customer receipt is captured and allocated.
3. Receivable visibility is reviewed in AR control.
4. Ledger and bank-book positions are monitored for closure.
5. Sales return impact is recorded where applicable.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start by reviewing invoices, receipts, receivable gaps, and bank or ledger items that need financial closure.
2. Use Invoices and Receipts to keep billing and collection entries tied to the correct customer and document trail.
3. Review AR Desk before chasing customers so overdue items, unallocated receipts, and exceptions are visible in one place.
4. Use GL Desk and Bank / Cash Book to validate accounting impact before marking a case as financially complete.
5. Keep unresolved accounting blockers inside Josh so commercial and finance teams see the same status.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review invoices and receipt capture.
2. Check open receivables and allocation gaps.
3. Monitor bank / cash entries and ledger impact.
4. Resolve accounting exceptions affecting customer closure.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for invoices, receipts, or customer allocations that remain open without a clear next finance action.
- Check AR exceptions and banking impact before treating a case as financially closed.
- Avoid working from disconnected statements or private notes when the receivable trail is already in Josh.
- Capture recurring finance-control or reconciliation friction as pilot feedback.
