## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to manage aftermarket operations covering service cases, warranty / replacement flow, AMC activity, reverse logistics, and job-card support.

- Aftermarket Desk
- Warranty / Service Replacement / Field Return

- AMC / Preventive Maintenance

## Process Flow

1. Customer issue or service need enters aftermarket workflow.
2. Service / warranty case is tracked and processed.
3. RMA, return, or reverse logistics is managed where needed.
4. Refurb or rework is coordinated for returned material.
5. AMC and field service obligations are tracked.
6. Job cards support execution activity.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start in Aftermarket Desk and review warranty, replacement, AMC, and reverse-logistics cases that need action now.
2. Open the exact service or warranty record before acting so customer issue details, part history, and previous updates are visible.
3. Use the linked service screens for RMA, reverse logistics, refurb, and AMC activity so the case stays connected end to end.
4. When field execution or repair support is needed, use Job Cards from the same case context instead of creating a side trail.
5. Keep pending parts, customer approvals, and closure blockers updated in Josh so service status remains visible to all desks.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review Aftermarket Desk queues.
2. Track active warranty / service cases.
3. Monitor reverse logistics and rework progress.
4. Review AMC and field-service commitments.
5. Coordinate parts, execution, and closure actions.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for open service cases with missing part status, pending customer response, or unclear closure ownership.
- Check that RMA, refurb, AMC, and job-card activity remains tied to the same case context inside Josh.
- Avoid separate service follow-up lists that disconnect from the actual aftermarket record.
- Capture recurring service, replacement, or closure bottlenecks as pilot feedback.
