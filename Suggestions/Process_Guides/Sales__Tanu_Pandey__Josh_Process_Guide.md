## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to manage customer-facing commercial flow from enquiry visibility through quote creation, order confirmation, and dispatch handoff readiness.

## Primary Screens

- Sales Desk
- Leads
- Customers
- Quotes
- Sales Orders
- Dispatches

## Process Flow

1. Lead or customer requirement is captured and tracked.
2. Commercial discussion is converted into a quote.
3. Approved or won quote is converted into a sales order.
4. Sales order is checked for fulfilment readiness before dispatch.
5. Dispatch execution completes the commercial handoff.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start in Sales Desk and pick the lead, quote, or customer follow-up that needs action first.
2. Open the exact customer or lead record in Josh and confirm contact details, enquiry scope, and the current commercial stage.
3. Prepare or revise the quote inside Quotes so pricing, terms, and revision history stay connected to the same customer trail.
4. Once the customer confirms, move the case into Sales Orders and keep dispatch handoff visibility inside Josh.
5. If pricing approval, stock, or dispatch readiness is blocked, keep that blocker recorded in Josh instead of tracking it separately.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review open leads and active customer opportunities.
2. Prepare and refine quotes.
3. Track quotes awaiting approval or conversion.
4. Monitor sales orders ready for dispatch handoff.
5. Check delivery status and cross-desk blockers.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for incomplete customer details, weak enquiry notes, or missing commercial context before you issue a quote.
- Check whether pricing approval, stock commitment, or dispatch readiness is blocking the next step.
- Avoid running a personal follow-up tracker that drifts away from the live Josh record.
- Capture repeated customer or quote-stage pain points as pilot feedback after real usage.

## Simple One-Line Summary

Josh helps Sales move enquiries into quotes and confirmed orders without losing follow-up visibility.
