## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to validate engineering-side product and BOM definition, manufacturing support readiness, and technical ownership for downstream execution.

## Primary Screens

- Manufacturing BOMs
- Job Cards
- Stock Ledger
- Product Master

## Process Flow

1. Product definition and engineering structure are reviewed.
2. BOM ownership and release readiness are validated.
3. Manufacturing handoff support is checked through job-card context.
4. Technical data and structure visibility support operations.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start by reviewing Product Master, Manufacturing BOMs, and open technical gaps affecting release or downstream execution.
2. Use Product Master to confirm item definition, specs, and structure before supporting manufacturing or commercial follow-up.
3. Use Manufacturing BOMs to validate release readiness, component structure, and engineering ownership against the exact product record.
4. Review Job Cards and Stock Ledger when production support issues point to definition, release, or structure problems.
5. Keep technical blockers, missing data, and release notes inside Josh so manufacturing and service teams see the same engineering position.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review Product Master and BOM definition quality.
2. Check engineering release readiness for production support.
3. Monitor technical gaps affecting manufacturing execution.
4. Coordinate structure and definition changes where required.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for incomplete product definition, weak BOM structure, or release gaps before production depends on the record.
- Check whether manufacturing support requests are tied back to the correct product and structure context in Josh.
- Avoid technical clarifications living only in calls or chat when downstream desks need the same reference trail.
- Capture repeated definition, release, or technical-visibility issues as pilot feedback.
