## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to review BOM structure, job-card execution, and inventory-backed production support from planning through material consumption visibility.

## Primary Screens

- Manufacturing BOMs
- Job Cards
- Stock Ledger

## Process Flow

1. Finished-good BOM is defined and maintained.
2. Job cards are raised for execution.
3. Material issue and consumption are tracked against jobs.
4. Production support gaps are surfaced for cross-desk action.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start with Manufacturing BOMs and open job-related production work that is waiting for release, structure check, or material visibility.
2. Use Job Cards to track execution status, issue readiness, and shop-floor support requirements against the active production record.
3. Review Stock Ledger when material availability or consumption must be confirmed before execution continues.
4. Keep BOM gaps, release blockers, and consumption concerns linked to Josh records so production support stays traceable.
5. Use Josh as the live execution board instead of relying on verbal follow-up for job progress and shortages.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review BOM readiness for active items.
2. Track open job cards and release blockers.
3. Check issue, return, and consumption visibility.
4. Flag shortage or setup gaps to upstream teams.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for BOM gaps, unreleased structures, or production blockers that should be visible before execution starts.
- Check whether job progress and material concerns are being recorded in Josh instead of passed verbally.
- Watch for repeated shortages or unclear issue status that will slow production flow.
- Capture pilot feedback on execution visibility, release readiness, and shop-floor support clarity.

## Simple One-Line Summary

Josh helps Manufacturing track BOM readiness, job execution, and production support visibility in one flow.
