## What Josh Does For Your Desk

Use Josh to control the procure-to-pay cycle from requirement and ordering through receipt, quality resolution, invoice validation, and vendor payment readiness.

## Process Flow

1. Requirement becomes Purchase Requisition.
2. Approved demand becomes Purchase Order.
3. Supplier delivery becomes Goods Receipt.
4. Quality exception becomes claim or purchase return if required.
5. Supplier bill becomes Purchase Invoice.
6. Three-way match validates PO vs GRN vs Invoice.
7. Vendor payment is released after commercial and control checks.

## How To Operate In Josh

1. Start with new requisitions and convert approved demand into Purchase Orders inside Josh.
2. Use Purchase Orders to track supplier commitment dates, pending deliveries, and commercial follow-up against the same record.
3. When material arrives, verify the receipt in Goods Receipts and watch for quality hold, claim, or return conditions before moving ahead.
4. Use Purchase Invoices and 3-way Match to spot PO, GRN, and bill differences before passing the case onward.
5. Record supplier delays, shortages, and pending clarifications in Josh so the next user sees the same status without a separate tracker.

## How You Should Operate Daily

1. Review new and submitted requisitions.
2. Convert approved demand into POs.
3. Monitor undelivered or ageing POs.
4. Post inward receipts and review QC exceptions.
5. Validate purchase invoices and match exceptions.
6. Track payment-ready vendors and statement closure.

## What To Watch Carefully

- Watch for pending supplier confirmation, overdue delivery, or receipt mismatch against the same PO.
- Check quality hold, claim, and return conditions before letting a case move forward.
- Avoid splitting supplier status across calls, chats, and offline sheets when the PO is already live in Josh.
- Capture repeated vendor-side or inward-side issues as pilot improvement feedback.
